Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goog PhotoFrame ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

If you choose to connect your Google account, the App may access Google Photos content and account information permitted by the permissions you grant. This access is used to provide the photo display features you request.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google services is used to provide the App's functionality, such as displaying your photos on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Storage and Sharing

The App is designed to display your photos for your personal use. Do not assume that information is never stored or transmitted: how data is processed depends on the App's actual implementation and the services you use. We do not share Google account information with third parties for their own advertising.

4. Google Services

When you use Google sign-in or Google Photos features, your use is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. The actual retention and deletion of data depend on how the App stores information and on the services involved.

6. Your Choices

You can disconnect your Google account or revoke the App's access through your Google Account permissions settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the App developer through the support channel provided with the App.
